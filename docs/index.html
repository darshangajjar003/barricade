    Set the table

    Up to four players share this screen and take turns. Switch any seat to the computer.

    How to play

    • Goal. Be the first pawn to step onto the crown in the centre of the 11×11 board.
    • Your turn. Either move your pawn one square (up, down, left, right) or place one barricade. You can't pass.
    • Barricades are two squares long and sit in the grooves between squares. They can't overlap or cross, and they can never cut any player off from the crown.
    • Jumping. Next to another pawn, you can jump straight over it. If a barricade, the edge, or a second pawn is behind it, step diagonally beside it instead.
    • Turn timer (optional). Run out of time and your pawn takes one step toward the crown for you. Run out twice in a row and you're out.
    • Sudden death. From turn 200 no more barricades can be placed. At turn 400 the pawn closest to the crown wins.
