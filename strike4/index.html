How to play

  1. 1Take turns dropping one coin into a column.
  2. 2The coin falls down to the lowest empty space.
  3. 3Get 4 of your coins in a row: across, up and down, or slanted.
  4. 4First to 4 wins! If the board fills up, it's a tie.
